On a quiet spring morning, a tiny seed lay buried beneath the soil.
Every day, it watched worms crawl by and listened to the birds singing above. The seed dreamed of seeing the world, but it was afraid to grow."What if I fail?" it wondered.One day, a gentle raindrop whispered, "You'll never know
what you're capable of unless you try."The warm sun added, "Every great tree was once a tiny seed."Encouraged, the seed stretched its roots deep into the earth and pushed a small green shoot toward the sky. The journey wasn't easy.
Strong winds bent it, heavy rains soaked it, and hot summers tested its strength.But with each challenge, the little plant grew taller.Years passed, and the tiny seed became a magnificent tree. Birds built nests in its branches,
children played under its shade, and travelers rested beneath its leaves.Looking back, the tree smiled."I was afraid to grow," it thought, "but every challenge helped me become who I am."
